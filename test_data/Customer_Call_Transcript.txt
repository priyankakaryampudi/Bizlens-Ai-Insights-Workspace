Customer feedback call – sample transcript

Customer: We like the product, but delivery delays have become frustrating.
CS Lead: Has this affected renewal or cancellation decisions?
Customer: For some orders, yes. When the promised timeline is missed, our teams cannot plan.
CS Lead: Which product is most affected?
Customer: Mostly Cloud Suite orders, although we have seen delays elsewhere too.
CS Lead: Would faster resolution change your likelihood of continuing?
Customer: It would definitely help, especially if we can get reliable delivery dates.

Internal note:
Validate whether customers reporting delivery issues have higher cancellation rates. Do not assume the relationship is causal until customer-level data is checked.
